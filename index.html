Research & perspective

I am an undergraduate researcher in Earth and Environmental Sciences at Seoul National University. My work spans Earth observation and medical imaging AI, with a focus on multi-temporal SAR flood mapping, hyperspectral image analysis, and energy-efficient spiking neural networks. My research experience also includes geomicrobiology, mineral analysis, and biological image quantification.
